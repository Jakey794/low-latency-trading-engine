# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, pull requests, and
  reviews.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome good-faith questions and feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, and sharing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Please report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's
GitHub profile rather than in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed fairly
and handled with appropriate confidentiality. The maintainer may remove,
edit, or reject contributions or comments that violate this code of conduct.
